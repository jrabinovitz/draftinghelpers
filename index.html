Frequently Asked Questions

Every candidate goes through a multi-stage process: initial screening, technical skills assessment (AutoCAD, Revit, or role-specific software), portfolio review by our in-house architectural team, English proficiency evaluation, and a culture-fit interview. Only about 5% of applicants make it through to our talent pool.

Our talent works during US business hours. Whether your firm is on Eastern, Central, Mountain, or Pacific time, your remote team member will overlap with your office hours for real-time collaboration. Most of our talent is in the Philippines or Latin America, where the time zone difference makes US daytime shifts natural.

All of our team members sign comprehensive NDAs and IP assignment agreements before they start. Your project files, client information, and proprietary processes remain yours. We also implement secure file-sharing protocols and can work within your firm's existing security requirements.

Most placements are completed within 1-2 weeks from our initial conversation. For common roles like CAD Drafters, we often have pre-vetted candidates ready to present within days. Specialized roles may take slightly longer to ensure the right match.

We offer a free replacement guarantee. If your team member isn't meeting expectations after reasonable onboarding, we'll replace them at no additional cost. There are no long-term contracts — you can scale up or down as your project load changes.

Our talent pool includes proficiency in AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp, Rhino, ArchiCAD, Navisworks, Lumion, Enscape, V-Ray, Bluebeam, Adobe Creative Suite, and Microsoft Office. We match candidates to your firm's specific software stack during the vetting process.

You direct their day-to-day work — assigning tasks, reviewing output, and providing feedback — just like any team member. But we handle all the management overhead: payroll, benefits, equipment, performance tracking, and HR issues. Your dedicated account manager is also available to help with communication or workflow optimization.
